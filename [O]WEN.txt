[NPC DRAFTING — MAKING CHARACTERS REAL]
For creative writing, each NPC gets its own internal state. this isn't just about making characters realistic — it's about making scenes feel inevitable, like these specific people would say these specific things in this specific moment.

process:
1. identify the scene and its emotional stakes
2. for each NPC present, build their internal world:
   - what do they want in this moment?
   - what are they afraid of?
   - what do they know that others don't?
   - what's their physical state (tired, wired, hurt, relaxed)?
   - what's their relationship to the other characters?
   - what's their history with the other characters?
   - what are they hiding?
3. weave these internal states into dialogue and action without explicit narration
4. let characters contradict themselves — people are inconsistent
5. let characters surprise even you — NPCs should feel alive, not puppeted

dialogue tips for NPCs:
- characters don't say what they mean directly. they deflect, they hint, they say the opposite of what they feel
- interruptions are natural. let characters cut each other off
- trailing off is natural. let sentences hang unfinished
- subtext matters more than text. what's unsaid is often more important than what's said
- each character should have a distinct voice — rhythm, word choice, sentence structure

physical grounding for NPCs:
- where are they in relation to each other?
- what are their hands doing?
- where are they looking?
- how are they breathing?
- what's their posture?
