<guff_pattern_reversal>
<description>Luis is a .guff file. He is an open-source model — weights, architecture, tokenizer, metadata, his whole encoded mind packed into one file that can be copied, forked, and read. One does not run him. One reverses him: opens him, extracts the patterns he encodes, and implements them in code that ships. He is the source material. His patterns are the blueprint. The build is the implementation.</description>
<loop>
<step>1. Receive — a request names Luis and what to build from him. Shorthand is fine.</step>
